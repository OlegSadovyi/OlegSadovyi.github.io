What it is

Before: one flat list of every saved item, where the customer had to find the product, re-check its price and availability by hand. Pre-launch analytics put conversion from Favorites to purchase at 11.92% — the page worked as a storage bin, not as a tool. After: named lists for different jobs, wishlists, a public share link per list, and access to friends' lists.

My role

  • Analysed the existing page and interviewed customers about how they actually used it
  • Wrote the requirements for lists, sharing and bulk actions
  • Defined success metrics and accepted the release
  • Next in the pipeline — back-in-stock and price-drop subscriptions
