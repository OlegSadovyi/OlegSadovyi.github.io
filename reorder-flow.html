What it is

Customers buying cyclical goods — pet food, diapers, coffee, household chemicals — had to rebuild the basket from scratch every time. We put reordering where the intent already exists: in the empty cart, in order details, in search, and as a pop-up when the customer starts assembling a familiar set. The mechanic was not chosen by taste: a three-group A/B test compared a pop-up against opening the cart, and the pop-up won on every key metric.

My role

  • Wrote requirements for four entry points and the logic for surfacing past orders
  • Designed the A/B test: hypothesis, group split, primary and health metrics
  • Made the final call on the mechanic based on test results
  • Ran a second round focused on feature discovery
