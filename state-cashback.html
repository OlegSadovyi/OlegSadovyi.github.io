What it is

Ukraine's state «Winter Support» programme lets recipients spend the benefit only on certain product categories, and only with the «National Cashback» card. To make it usable we had to flag eligible products across the catalogue, give the programme its own entry point for people who don't know its internal name, surface the constraint before the cart rather than at payment, and add a separate payment method with basket-level validation.

My role

  • Requirements for every surface: menu, campaign landing, product page, checkout
  • Basket validation rules — an order may contain eligible products only
  • Aligned the mechanic with the partner bank and the finance team
  • Framed the constraints so customers learn them before they reach payment
